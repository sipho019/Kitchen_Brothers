OUR STORY

Made with flavour.
Served with heart.

Kitchen Brothers is a restaurant in Mthatha, Eastern Cape, serving a distinctive blend of Mexican-inspired cuisine and South African favourites.

From stacked burgers and loaded fries to kotas, wraps and wings, the menu is made for people who enjoy bold flavours and satisfying meals.

This website celebrates the restaurant's food, its team and the experience of bringing people together around good food.
